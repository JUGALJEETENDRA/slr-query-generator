How It Works
Phase 1 — Query Generation
1
✍️
Enter Research Question
Type your research question in the Query Generator tab. Be specific about the topic, method, context, and desired outcome for best results.
2
🧠
AI Extracts Key Concepts
AI automatically identifies the primary concept, domain context, and comparator terms from your question — no manual keyword picking needed.
3
🔍
Boolean Queries Generated
Optimized Boolean search strings with correct field-tag syntax are instantly built for all 5 databases — Google Scholar, Scopus, WoS, IEEE, PubMed.
↓
Phase 2 — Collect, Deduplicate & Screen
4
📋
Copy & Search Databases
Copy the query for each database and paste it directly into its search bar. Download the results as a CSV file from each platform.
5
🔗
LitSync — Merge & Deduplicate
Upload all exported CSVs to the LitSync tab. It auto-detects each database format, merges all files, and removes duplicate papers by DOI and title.
6
🤖
AI Screens Papers
Upload the clean LitSync dataset to the CSV Screener. AI reads each title and abstract and classifies the paper as Keep, Maybe, or Reject.
↓
7
📊
View Your PRISMA 2020 Flow Diagram
The backend-owned PRISMA diagram (above) updates during import, deduplication, automated screening, and manual MAYBE review. It reports records identified, actual removals, records screened, exclusions, pending review, and provisional title/abstract inclusions. Full-text eligibility and final study inclusion are deliberately not claimed.
